02 / About

A studio that engineers
fashion supply chains.

Ni-Yu Tex International was founded in 2016 as a Bangalore-based denim studio with one mandate: collapse the distance between a tech-pack and a finished pallet. Today our professionally managed team runs end-to-end programs for retail brands in India and across the overseas market.

  • ①

    Range building & product development

    Updated fashion pipelines with real-time forecast inputs from Crocker Jeans Paris.

  • ②

    Streamlined PD & sourcing

    Internal processes tuned for seamless supply chain solutions and 95% On-Time-In-Full.

  • ③

    Commercial value, lower op-cost

    Better product sourcing cost and reduced operational overhead, audited every quarter.

  • ④

    Re-engineered for productivity

    We rework raw material, processes & construction so the same garment costs less to make.

04 / Capabilities

Saving 95% blue water,
without losing the wash.

Our laundry inputs come from Ramdan, Fibro Organic, Jeanologia and Garmon-Italy — enabling us to cut chemical usage and recover almost all process water. Fabric development reaches from Candiani in Italy to every leading mill in India.

Compatibility runs both ways: we can serve a low MOQ of 500 pcs / wash optic for sampling waves and scale to 150,000 pcs / month with our dedicated outsourced facilities.
